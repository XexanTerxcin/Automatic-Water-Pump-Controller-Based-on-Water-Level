# Automatic-Water-Pump-Controller-Based-on-Water-Level


📌 Project Overview

This project implements an automatic water pump controller that turns a water pump ON or OFF based on the water level in a tank.
The system helps prevent water overflow, dry running, and manual effort, making it suitable for home and small-scale water management systems.

The controller uses water level sensing probes, a 555 timer IC, a relay-based switching stage, and proper electrical protection components to ensure safe and reliable operation.

⚙️ Working Principle

When the water level is LOW, the controller automatically turns ON the pump.

When the water level reaches FULL, the controller turns OFF the pump.

A main power switch allows the entire system to be enabled or disabled manually.

The pump is switched using a high-power relay, while the control circuit operates at low voltage for safety.

Water level is detected using conductive probes placed at different heights inside the tank. These signals are processed by the control circuit, which then drives the relay accordingly.

🔧 Key Components Used

NE555 Timer IC (used for control logic)

High-power SPDT relay (e.g., TN90 series)

NPN transistor (BC547) for relay driving

Flyback diode (1N4007) for relay coil protection

RC snubber (0.1µF X2 capacitor + 100Ω resistor) across motor/load

Water level probes

Low-voltage DC power supply

AC pump load (simulated in Proteus)

🛡️ Protection & Safety Features

Flyback diode protects the transistor from relay coil back-EMF.

RC snubber circuit suppresses voltage spikes generated by the inductive pump load.

Relay isolation keeps high-voltage AC separate from low-voltage control electronics.

Designed so that failure of the relay does not damage the control circuit.

🧪 Simulation

The circuit was simulated using Proteus.

Since Proteus does not accurately model single-phase pump motors, a lamp / resistive load was used to verify relay switching behavior.

Simulation focuses on logic correctness, not mechanical or thermal behavior of motors.
